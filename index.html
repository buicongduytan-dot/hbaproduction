About the Studio

Born in
Việt Nam,
built for the world.

HBA Production is a full-service creative studio based in Hồ Chí Minh City. We partner with brands, agencies, and enterprises to create visual content that earns attention — filmmaking, photography, design, and marketing strategy executed to international standards.

Every project is approached with one intent: to make something that outlasts the brief. From a single TVC to an end-to-end brand identity, we bring precision and purpose to every frame.

How We Work

The production process

01

Brief & Discovery

We listen before we speak. A deep-dive into your brand, objectives, and audience to define the creative territory and the right approach.

02

Pre-Production

Concept development, scripting, location scouting, casting, and production design. Every detail confirmed before a camera rolls.

03

Production

Principal photography with our own crew and equipment. Precise direction, technical excellence, and real-time client communication.

04

Post & Delivery

Editing, colour grading, sound design, and motion in our own post facility — until the final master is exactly right.
